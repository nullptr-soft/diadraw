description: How to reach the people who make DiaDraw.
---

DiaDraw is developed in the open on GitHub.

- **Bugs, ideas and questions** go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/mykulyak/diadraw/issues),
  where they can be discussed and followed. The [feedback](../feedback/) page has forms that ask
  for what we need to help.
- **The code** is at [github.com/mykulyak/diadraw](https://github.com/mykulyak/diadraw), with the
  design notes and the full documentation.
- **The maintainer** is [@mykulyak](https://github.com/mykulyak) on GitHub.
